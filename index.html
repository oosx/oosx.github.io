Wu Kechen

I am interested in climate variability and extreme weather events, with a particular focus on temperature extremes and intraseasonal variability. My research uses long-term observational records, statistical analysis, and data-driven methods to better understand how extreme events evolve across time and space. I am also interested in applying computational approaches to environmental and atmospheric research.
